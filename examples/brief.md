# Project brief: Tasty App (sample)

## Problem
Small restaurants take table reservations by phone and lose bookings outside opening hours.

## Target users
- Diners booking a table from a phone or laptop.
- Restaurant staff managing the day's reservations.

## Goals and KPIs
- 30% of reservations made online within 3 months of launch.
- Staff confirm or decline a request in under 2 minutes.

## Must-have features
- Diner: see available slots, request a booking, receive confirmation by email.
- Staff: daily reservation list, confirm/decline, block time slots.

## Out of scope
- Payments and deposits.
- Native mobile apps.

## Constraints
- Deadline: first release within 8 weeks.
- Budget: see arima.yaml.
- Platforms: web (responsive).

## Known risks
- Email deliverability for confirmations.
- Double booking under concurrent requests.
